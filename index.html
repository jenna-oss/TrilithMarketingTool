Plan a batch of concepts

Say how many short-form videos you want and the agent proposes that many concepts, each pairing a hook form borrowed from outside the category with a topic drawn from competitor ads and Trilith’s own writing. No two share a hook or a topic. You drop whatever you do not want, and nothing is locked until you confirm the set.

Questions about the underlying data live on the briefing, where the ads and the numbers are on screen next to the answer.

Each concept is a hook and a topic for one vertical video, with the opening line written out. Review the set, drop what you do not want, then lock it for production.
