How this works

Ask a question about the category — who is running what, how a claim is phrased, how many advertisers use it. The agent searches before it answers and shows you every search it ran. Counts come from the whole corpus rather than a sample, so "how many" and "who does it most" are exact.

Planning a batch is the other half. Say how many short-form videos you want and the agent proposes that many concepts, each pairing a hook form borrowed from outside the category with a topic drawn from competitor ads and Trilith's own writing. No two share a hook or a topic. You drop whatever you do not want, and nothing is locked until you confirm the set.

Each concept is a hook and a topic for one vertical video, with the opening line written out. Review the set, drop what you do not want, then lock it for production.
