Before this can connect

  1. Sign up at www.upload-post.com (with the www — the bare address can hang). TikTok needs a paid plan; the others work on any.
  2. Copy the API key from its dashboard.
  3. From the worker folder: npx wrangler secret put UPLOAD_POST_API_KEY, then deploy.

Before this can connect

  1. At developers.facebook.com, create a Business app owned by the Trilith Funding Business Portfolio, and add Facebook Login for Business to it.
  2. Register …/meta/callback as a Valid OAuth Redirect URI, exactly.
  3. Make sure the Instagram account is linked to the Facebook Page in the Business Portfolio. It has to be a Professional account.
  4. From the worker folder, set three secrets: META_APP_ID, META_APP_SECRET and META_TOKEN_KEY, then deploy.
