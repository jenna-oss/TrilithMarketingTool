Connect the Instagram account once, and posts can go out from here at a time you choose. You sign in on Instagram's own page — this app never sees the password, only a token it can publish with, which it keeps encrypted.

Before this can connect

  1. At developers.facebook.com, create an app and add Instagram to it.
  2. Register …/instagram/callback as an OAuth redirect URI, exactly.
  3. Add the Instagram account as a tester, and accept the invitation in that account's settings. It has to be a Professional account.
  4. From the worker folder, set three secrets: INSTAGRAM_APP_ID, INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET and INSTAGRAM_TOKEN_KEY, then deploy.

Next: picking a finished video, writing the caption with its script beside you, and choosing when it goes out. Images and carousels after that. Nothing posts until you connect the account above.
