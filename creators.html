Single-person educators, not brand ads

Trilith wants to make educational content, not ads. The competitor ad corpus on the briefing is optimised for a click, and its hook forms are built to interrupt a scroll, not to teach anything. These five people are the other model: individual educators in residential real estate investing whose organic content is built to be watched to the end, because the payoff is understanding something, not a claim.

Study the form here — the ranking, the worked example, the plain-numbers walkthrough — the same way the hook-pattern library is studied for ad structure. This is not the competitor-ad briefing, and nothing here is a lender Trilith competes with.

How this was gathered, and what it is not

Found via a Spyglass creator search on 2026-08-17 and hand-checked one by one — several names that came back were dropped for being off-thesis (a realtor's personal brand, land investing, the wrong market and language). This is a one-time research pass, not on the daily or weekly refresh the rest of the site runs on, and it is committed as a plain data file rather than a database table.
