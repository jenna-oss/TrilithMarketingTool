Hook taxonomy

The eleven angles the category runs on

Ordered by how heavily the category leans on each one. The last two came from outside the primary corpus.

01 Deal-loss urgency Kiavi, Lima One
“Close before others even get approved”
“Compete like a cash buyer”
02 First-person financing confession Kiavi — 4 variants live

Admit the fear, name financing as the villain, resolve with the brand.

03 Volume flex as proof Kiavi
“I had 3 closings in one month and Kiavi funded all of them.”
04 Myth-bust / contrarian correction New Silver
“Stop comparing lenders on rate alone.”
05 Stage transition — you did X, now do Y Lima One — 4 ads on one grammar
“You fixed it. Now rent it.”
06 Legacy-system callout Kiavi
“Your builds are modern. So why is your financing stuck in the past?”
07 Betrayal / bait-and-switch Visio

The most aggressive line anyone is running.

08 Identity mirroring Visio

Flatter the operator’s craft, then indict their financing for not matching it.

09 Cost-of-error education Kiavi, New Silver — organic
“Small comp mistakes cost big money — $40K swings.”
10 The broker’s commission, not the borrower’s deal RCN — paid, invisible to Spyglass
“Don’t let a slow lender cost your client the deal — or your commission.”
11 Show the arithmetic Creators only

Line-item deal math, no claim attached. The single best-performing format in this audience — and no lender uses it.
